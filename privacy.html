Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly stated.

Pericope is made by Chandler Dowd. This policy explains what the app handles, why it handles it, and the choices currently available to you.

Effective November 17, 2026

Pericope does not use accounts or advertising

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not include advertising, sell personal information, or use an advertising identifier. Pericope does not include an analytics service for tracking your listening behavior.

Information stored on your device

To provide progress, recently played passages, and a resume point, Pericope stores listening history on your device. This includes passage identifiers, the dates passages were played or completed, and play counts. The app also stores playback preferences, such as speed, repeat mode, and the last selected passage.

These records are not sent by Pericope to a listening-history server. They may be included in an iPhone or iPad backup depending on your device settings. In the current version, deleting the app removes its local app data; device backups are controlled by Apple and your backup settings.

Scripture requests and narration

When a Bible chapter is not available in Pericope’s current session cache, the app requests that chapter from Pericope’s chapter service, which obtains World English Bible text through API.Bible. These requests identify the requested chapter. Network infrastructure, the hosting provider, and API.Bible may process routine connection information needed to deliver the request, such as IP-address-related network data.

Pericope can download an optional Pocket TTS voice model to your device and generate narration on-device. The model is stored in the app’s local storage. The app can also use an installed iOS system voice.

Support email

If you email chandler.dowd+pericope-support@gmail.com, Chandler receives the information you include, along with your email address, through Gmail. That information is used to respond to your request and maintain support correspondence.

Choices and questions

Because Pericope does not maintain user accounts or a server-side listening-history profile, it cannot retrieve or delete listening history held only on your device. You can remove local app data by deleting the app. For questions about this policy or your data, contact chandler.dowd+pericope-privacy@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If Pericope’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant app update. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision.
