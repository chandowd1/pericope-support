Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly stated.

Pericope is made by Chandler Dowd. This policy explains what the app handles, why it handles it, and the choices currently available to you.

Effective November 24, 2026

Pericope does not use accounts, advertising or analytics

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not include advertising, sell personal information, or use an advertising identifier. It does not include an analytics or tracking service, and it does not send your listening history anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To provide progress, your streak, recently played chapters, and a place to resume, Pericope stores listening history on your device. This includes passage identifiers, the dates passages were played or completed, play counts, and a record of completed chapters. The app also stores your playback settings, such as speed, repeat mode, whether commentary is on, and the last passage you selected.

This information is not sent to any server operated by Pericope. It stays in the app’s private storage on your iPhone or iPad and is included in your device backups according to your iOS backup settings (for example iCloud Backup or a backup made to a computer).

Deleting your listening history

You can delete your listening history at any time in the app: open Reading Progress and choose the trash button, then confirm. This removes your progress, streak, recently played chapters, and saved place from the device. Your playback settings and the downloaded narrator are kept. Deleting the app removes all of the app’s data from the device.

Deleting history cannot erase backups that were already made. A backup made earlier stays as it was until a newer backup replaces it: your iCloud backup is replaced the next time your device backs up to iCloud, and a backup stored on a computer is replaced only when that computer is backed up again.

Scripture, commentary and narration

The World English Bible text and Matthew Henry’s Concise Commentary are included in the app, so reading and listening to them does not require an internet connection and does not send the chapters you choose to anyone.

Pericope can narrate with a neural voice that runs on your device. The first time you choose it, the app asks before downloading the voice model, which is about 165 MB. If you agree, the model is downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a third-party host. Hugging Face and the network providers between you and it can receive routine connection information such as your IP address and the files requested, and handle it under their own policies. Nothing is downloaded from Hugging Face unless you choose to download it. If you decline, the app uses the built-in iOS system voice. After the download, narration is generated on your device.

Links to other sites

The About & Resources screen links to the sources of the text, commentary, and voice technology. When you open one of those links, your browser or the linked site’s own privacy policy applies.

TestFlight, App Store and support email

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to submit with the developer, according to your device settings. If you email chandler.dowd+pericope-support@gmail.com, Chandler receives the information you include, along with your email address, through Gmail. That information is used to respond to your request and maintain support correspondence.

Choices and questions

Because Pericope does not maintain accounts or a server-side profile, there is no remote copy of your listening history for the app to retrieve or delete; you control it on your device as described above. For questions about this policy or your data, contact chandler.dowd+pericope-privacy@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If Pericope’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant app update. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision.
