Support

A quiet place to get help.

Pericope is a Scripture listening app with World English Bible chapters and Matthew Henry’s Concise Commentary. If something is not working, or you have an idea, Chandler would be glad to hear from you.

Email support

For help, feedback, or a feature request, email:

chandler.dowd+pericope-support@gmail.com

If you can, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information.

Before you write

  • Chapter text and commentary are included in the app and work without an internet connection.
  • The optional on-device narrator is a one-time download of about 165 MB that the app asks about first. Until it is downloaded, or if you decline, the built-in iOS system voice is used.
  • You can delete your listening history from the Reading Progress screen. Earlier device backups are not erased until a newer backup replaces them.
  • Source and narrator details are available in Pericope under About & Resources.
